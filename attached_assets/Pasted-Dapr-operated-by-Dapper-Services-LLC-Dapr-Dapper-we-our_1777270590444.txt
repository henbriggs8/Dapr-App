1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, information collected automatically, and information from third-party services that help us operate Dapr.

Information You Provide

When you create an account, book a service, contact support, or use Dapr, we may collect:

* Name
* Phone number
* Email address
* Vehicle information, such as year, make, model, color, license plate, or photos if provided
* Service address or appointment location
* Booking details, including selected service, add-ons, appointment time, and special instructions
* Payment-related information
* Customer support messages
* Reviews, ratings, notes, or feedback
* Promo code, referral, or membership information

Location and Service Address Information

Dapr uses location information to provide mobile car care services, match customers with available service providers, show service availability, estimate arrival times, and complete bookings.

We may collect:

* The address you enter for a service
* Approximate location based on your device or browser, if you allow it
* Service provider location while they are actively completing or traveling to a job
* Appointment-related location history, such as pickup, arrival, and completion status

You may disable device location permissions in your device settings, but some features may not work properly without location access.

Payment Information

Payments may be processed by third-party payment providers, including Square. We do not store full credit card numbers on our own systems. Payment processors may collect and process payment information, billing details, fraud-prevention information, and transaction history according to their own privacy policies.

Information Collected Automatically

When you use Dapr, we may automatically collect:

* Device type
* Browser type
* IP address
* App usage activity
* Pages or screens viewed
* Booking flow activity
* Error logs
* Analytics data
* Referral source
* Approximate location derived from IP address
* Cookies or similar tracking technologies

This information helps us improve the app, prevent fraud, troubleshoot issues, and understand how customers use Dapr.

Information From Service Providers

If you are a detailer, contractor, employee, franchise partner, or service provider using Dapr, we may collect additional information, including:

* Name
* Phone number
* Email address
* Profile photo
* Vehicle or equipment information
* Work availability
* Service history
* Ratings and customer feedback
* Job location while actively using the service provider side of the platform
* Payout or tax-related information, if applicable
* Background, insurance, certification, or eligibility information, if required

With Technology Providers

We may share information with companies that help us operate Dapr, including providers for:

With Franchisees, Affiliates, or Business Partners

We may disclose information if required to do so by law, court order, subpoena, legal process, or government request. We may also disclose information when we believe it is necessary to protect our rights, property, users, service providers, or the public.
