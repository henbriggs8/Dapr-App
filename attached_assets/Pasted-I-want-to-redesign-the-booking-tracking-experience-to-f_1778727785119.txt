I want to redesign the booking/tracking experience to feel MUCH more alive and real-time like Uber, DoorDash, or Instacart — not like a traditional appointment booking app.

The goal is for the customer to feel:
- progress is constantly happening
- the app is actively working for them
- a Detail Pro is being sourced in real time
- the experience feels instant/on-demand
- high trust + low anxiety

Right now the experience feels static and empty.

I want a hybrid system:
1. Dynamic “finding your Detail Pro” flow before assignment
2. Live map tracking once assigned
3. Service progress tracker once service starts

IMPORTANT:
The UI should feel premium, modern, animated, and alive.
Think:
- Uber
- DoorDash
- Instacart
- Waymo
NOT:
- old booking software
- appointment scheduler
- static forms

-----------------------------------
PHASE 1 — MATCHING FLOW
-----------------------------------

After customer confirms booking, immediately transition into a full-screen live activity state.

Show:
- animated searching state
- nearby Detail Pros being sourced
- ETA estimates changing slightly
- subtle map movement/background animation
- pulsing loading indicators
- status text cycling naturally

I want rotating intelligent status messages like:

- Finding nearby Detail Pros…
- Connecting you with the best Detail Pro…
- Checking availability nearby…
- Looking for the fastest arrival…
- Matching you with a top-rated Detail Pro…
- Optimizing arrival time…
- Searching your area…
- Finding someone close…
- Preparing your service…
- Locking in your arrival window…
- Confirming equipment availability…
- Building the best route…
- Finalizing your Detail Pro…

These should rotate naturally every few seconds with smooth fade/slide transitions.

The experience should feel:
- active
- intelligent
- real-time
- exciting

NOT fake or cheesy.

-----------------------------------
PHASE 2 — DETAIL PRO ASSIGNED
-----------------------------------

Once assigned:
Transition smoothly into a live map view.

Show:
- live Detail Pro location
- moving vehicle icon
- live ETA
- Detail Pro photo
- vehicle info
- distance away
- estimated arrival time
- call/message buttons
- smooth map animations

The transition should feel seamless and premium.

Very similar emotional feel to:
- Uber driver assigned
- DoorDash dasher en route

-----------------------------------
PHASE 3 — SERVICE PROGRESS
-----------------------------------

Once the Detail Pro arrives:
Switch from navigation-focused UI into service progress UI.

Use a clean progress tracker with animated states like:

1. Arrived
2. Setting Up Equipment
3. Exterior Wash In Progress
4. Interior Detail In Progress
5. Final Touches
6. Quality Check
7. Service Complete

Each stage should:
- animate smoothly
- feel satisfying
- reassure the customer that progress is happening

Optionally show:
- estimated remaining time
- live updated completion estimate
- add-on alerts (pet hair, stains, XL vehicle, etc.)

-----------------------------------
DESIGN STYLE
-----------------------------------

Design language should feel:
- minimal
- premium
- tech-forward
- slightly futuristic
- clean typography
- smooth animations
- soft gradients
- subtle motion
- no clutter

Avoid:
- giant empty white space
- boring loaders
- generic booking software vibes

Focus heavily on:
- motion
- responsiveness
- perceived speed
- reassurance
- premium UX psychology

The customer should constantly feel:
“Something is happening right now.”
