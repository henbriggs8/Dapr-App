Compensation: approved 65/35 policy

Provider earnings are 65% of the full saved service quote subtotal (including quoted add-ons), rounded half-up to integer cents. Dapr receives the remainder and funds promotions/referral credits. Existing finalized earnings, including zero, are preserved; completed historical jobs are never backfilled or repriced. A missing/broken quote prevents new finalization instead of falling back to the discounted customer charge. Supported legacy estimates use catalog service plus saved add-ons.

Example: $149.00 subtotal → $96.85 Provider, $52.15 Dapr before discounts. With a platform-funded 99% discount the Provider still receives $96.85; Dapr net is negative. Customer payment fields, Stripe intent IDs and payment status are unchanged by earnings finalization. This is accounting behavior, not an automated Stripe transfer/payout system. Tips and Stripe settlement reconciliation remain subject to the existing separate flows.

Tests cover saved-quote precedence over changed catalog prices, referral/promo funding, deterministic cents, persisted zero/history, concurrent completion, idempotent retries, duration estimates versus elapsed time, available/assigned job estimates and read-only reporting. No existing customer/provider balances or payments were modified.
